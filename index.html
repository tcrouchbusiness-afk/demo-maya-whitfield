04 About

I moved to Lockeland Springs in 2019 and never left.

I bought a 1920s bungalow on Holly Street in 2019, put a new roof on it, and learned the east side one block at a time. Since 2021 I've helped 38 households buy or sell between the river and Gallatin Pike, plus a growing handful in Germantown and 12 South, which is where my east-side clients go when they want a walk to a coffee shop and a smaller yard.

I'm not a team. You get me on the phone, at the showing, and at the closing table. I'll tell you which streets flood in a hard rain, which blocks back up to the rail line, and which "renovated" listing still has knob-and-tube in the attic.

05 Buying

Buying on the east side

Most of my buyers are moving across town or across the country and have three weekends to figure out a neighborhood I've spent seven years in. So we don't tour twenty houses. We tour six, and I tell you what I'd pay for each one and why.

  1. A thirty-minute call about where you actually spend your week.
  2. A shortlist of streets, with what each one costs.
  3. Showings where I bring the flood map and look in the attic.
  4. An offer priced to the comps, not the list price.

06 Selling

Selling an east-side house

Your house is worth what the last three comparable sales on your block say it is, adjusted for what you've done to it. I'll show you those three sales, tell you the number, and tell you what two weekends of work would add to it.

  1. A walk-through and a written price with the comps attached.
  2. A short list of fixes that return more than they cost.
  3. Professional photos, one open house, and a list price that draws offers in the first week.
  4. Negotiation on inspection items so the deal doesn't die on a water heater.

08 Questions I get

East Nashville, Germantown, or 12 South?

East Nashville if you want a yard, a porch, and the most house for the money. Germantown if you want to walk to dinner and don't need a yard. 12 South if you want both and can pay for it.

What's the commute from East Nashville to downtown?

About ten minutes by car over the Woodland Street bridge, or twenty to twenty-five by bike on the Shelby Bottoms greenway.

How do I find out what my house is worth?

Text me the address. I'll pull the last three comparable sales on your block and send you a number with the comps attached, usually the same day.

Do you work with first-time buyers?

Yes, and I'll spend the first call on lending and what monthly payment you're comfortable with before we look at a single listing.

What's the price range you work in?

Most of my closings are between $350,000 and $1,200,000. I'm glad to work above or below that; the neighborhoods I know run the whole range.

Are you on a team?

No. You get me at the showing, on the phone, and at closing.
