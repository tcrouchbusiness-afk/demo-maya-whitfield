Text Maya about 12 South

12 South is the half-mile of 12th Avenue South between Sevier Park and Wedgewood Avenue, plus the residential blocks on either side of it. It's ten minutes from downtown, eight from Vanderbilt, and it has more coffee shops per block than anywhere else in the city. When my east-side clients say they want to be able to walk somewhere every evening, this is where we look, and I tell them up front that it costs more.

What the housing looks like

The core blocks are 1920s and 1930s cottages and bungalows, most of them renovated at least once and many of them expanded. North on Belmont Boulevard the houses get bigger; the five-bedroom at 2904 Belmont Boulevard, listed at $3,790,000 on this page, is a Victoria Place house. Northeast in Edgehill you'll find townhome rows and new builds, which is where most of the four-bedrooms under $1,000,000 are. South of Sevier Park toward Woodmont, in Waverly-Belmont, lots get larger and the street noise drops.

What it costs

On September 7, 2026, RealTracs showed 159 active listings in the 12 South market area with an average list price of $1,327,452 and an average of $482 per square foot. The range that day ran from $165,000 to $5,299,000. The listings on this page include a four-bedroom Edgehill townhome at $824,990, a three-bedroom on 9th Avenue South at $1,049,000, and a two-bedroom condo at The Enclave at Hillsboro Village at $485,000. A renovated three-bedroom cottage on the core blocks generally lands between $1,000,000 and $1,700,000.

Getting around

12th Avenue South runs straight north into downtown in about ten minutes. Vanderbilt and the medical center are eight minutes west via Wedgewood. The airport is about eighteen minutes using I-440 to I-40. Most of the neighborhood is within a fifteen-minute walk of Sevier Park.

Sevier Park and the strip

Sevier Park is the green anchor at the south end: ballfields, a community center, and the 12 South Farmers Market on Sunday afternoons in season. The strip itself is coffee (Frothy Monkey, White Bison, Portland Brew), dinner (Edley's Bar-B-Que, Bartaco, Bottle Cap), and boutiques (MODA, Draper James, the shops that turn over every couple of years). The 'I Believe in Nashville' mural on 12th is a tourist stop; on Saturday afternoons there's a line.

What to watch for

Parking, first. The side streets near the strip fill up, and a house without a driveway or alley access is a different daily life than one with. Second, additions: many cottages here have been expanded upward or backward, and I want to see the permits. Third, what the listing calls 12 South. The market area stretches into Edgehill, Belmont-Hillsboro, and Hillsboro Village, and prices differ by block; I'll tell you which one you're actually looking at.

Neighborhoods and pockets

  • 12 South proper The blocks either side of 12th Avenue South between Sevier Park and Wedgewood. Cottages, renovated bungalows, and the highest prices per foot.
  • Belmont-Hillsboro North toward Belmont University. Bigger 1920s houses on Belmont Boulevard; the $3,790,000 listing on this page is here.
  • Edgehill Northeast toward downtown. Townhomes and new builds; the $824,990 four-bedroom on 14th Avenue South is an Edgehill townhome.
  • Waverly-Belmont South of Sevier Park toward Woodmont. Quieter, larger lots, a step down in price per foot.
  • Hillsboro Village edge West across 21st Avenue. The $485,000 two-bedroom condo at The Enclave is the entry point.

Questions about 12 South

What does 12 South cost?

As of September 7, 2026, RealTracs showed 159 active listings averaging $1,327,452, at an average of $482 per square foot, with prices from $165,000 for a small condo to $5,299,000. A renovated three-bedroom on the core blocks generally lands between $1,000,000 and $1,700,000.

Why is 12 South so much more than East Nashville?

Smaller area, less inventory, and proximity to Vanderbilt, Belmont, and Green Hills. It's a ten-minute drive to downtown and eight to Vanderbilt, and there is only so much of it.

Where do people park?

On the side streets, and it's a sore subject. If you're buying on 12th itself or the first block off it, ask about a driveway or an alley garage.

What schools serve 12 South?

Metro Nashville Public Schools. The RealTracs market page lists Waverly-Belmont Elementary, Eakin Elementary, John Trotwood Moore Middle, West End Middle, and Hillsboro High. Zoning depends on the exact address.

Are there condos?

A few, mostly on the edges: The Enclave at Hillsboro Village had a two-bedroom at $485,000 on the day I wrote this. Inside the core it's houses and a handful of townhome rows.
