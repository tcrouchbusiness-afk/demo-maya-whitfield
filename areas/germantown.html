Text Maya about Germantown

Germantown is Nashville's oldest neighborhood and one of its smallest: roughly the grid between Jefferson Street on the south, Hume Street on the north, 3rd Avenue on the east, and Rosa L. Parks Boulevard on the west, according to the RealTracs market page. It's a five-minute drive to downtown and a twenty-minute walk. Most of my clients who end up here started their search on the east side and decided they'd rather have a stoop and a restaurant on the corner than a yard.

What the housing looks like

Three things coexist on the same block. Restored 19th-century brick houses, some of the oldest residential buildings in the city. Townhomes built since about 2005, usually three stories with a garage on the ground floor. And, along the western and southern edges toward the North Gulch, larger new construction; the five-bedroom on 11th Avenue North listed at $2,098,000 on this page is that type. Salemtown, just north of Hume, is where the cottages and smaller new builds are, and where first-time buyers usually land.

What it costs

On September 7, 2026, RealTracs showed 32 active listings in Germantown. The for-sale side ran from $775,000 for a three-bedroom townhome on 6th Avenue North to $2,399,900 for a five-bedroom, four-bath house on 5th Avenue North. A four-bedroom on Scovel Street listed at $859,000. The rental side of the same feed ran from $1,800 for a one-bedroom to $5,000 a month for a three-bedroom townhome.

Getting around

Rosa L. Parks Boulevard takes you to the Capitol and Broadway in five minutes. Jefferson Street runs west toward Fisk and Tennessee State. The airport is about seventeen minutes on I-40 East. The Cumberland River Greenway starts at the ballpark and runs along the river toward downtown for walkers and cyclists.

The market, the park, the ballpark

The Nashville Farmers' Market on Rosa Parks is open every day. The market house holds a dozen small restaurants and the outdoor sheds hold the produce stands; on Saturday mornings it's the busiest place in the neighborhood. Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park is the long lawn running from the market up toward the Capitol, with the Tennessee State Museum at its north end. First Horizon Park, home of the Nashville Sounds, sits on the west edge; games run April through September and parking on those nights is worth asking about before you buy on that side.

Restaurants

Rolf and Daughters on Taylor Street, 5th & Taylor a block away, Butchertown Hall and Germantown Café on 5th Avenue, Bearded Iris Brewing at the north end. Most of them are within ten minutes of each other on foot.

What to watch for

HOA documents on every townhome, because nearly all of them have one and the fees vary widely. Deeded parking versus street parking, because the streets fill up. And on the historic brick houses, foundation and moisture; these are 130-year-old buildings on a floodplain-adjacent grid, and a good inspector earns their fee here.

Neighborhoods and pockets

  • Historic Germantown The original grid between Jefferson Street and Hume Street, 3rd Avenue to Rosa L. Parks. Restored brick, new townhomes, and the restaurants.
  • Salemtown Just north of Hume. Cottages and new builds, a step down in price, and the most first-time buyers.
  • North Gulch edge The southwest corner toward 11th Avenue N, where the larger new construction sits. The $2,098,000 listing on this page is here.
  • Along the river Condos and townhomes near First Horizon Park and the Cumberland greenway.

Questions about Germantown

What do homes in Germantown cost?

The for-sale listings on RealTracs as of September 2026 ran from $775,000 for a three-bedroom townhome to $2,399,900 for a five-bedroom on 5th Avenue North. Rentals in the same feed listed between $1,800 and $5,000 a month. There were 32 active listings that day, so inventory is thin.

Is Germantown walkable?

Yes. It's a short grid; the farmers' market, the ballpark, and the main restaurants are all within about ten minutes on foot from anywhere inside Jefferson, Hume, 3rd, and Rosa Parks. Downtown is a five-minute drive or a twenty-minute walk.

What schools serve Germantown?

Metro Nashville Public Schools. The RealTracs market page lists Jones Paideia Magnet (elementary), John Early Paideia Magnet (middle), and Pearl-Cohn or Martin Luther King Jr. Magnet (high). Magnet admission is by application; check MNPS for zoning by address.

Townhome or single-family?

Mostly townhomes and condos. Detached houses exist on the historic grid and command a premium; expect an HOA on nearly all new construction.

What's parking like?

Tight, and tighter on Sounds game nights. Ask whether a listing has deeded or garage parking before you count on street spots.
